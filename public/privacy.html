Privacy Policy

Maya Computers. Last updated 31 October 2026.

This policy explains how Maya Computers Books, the shop's internal record keeping system, handles personal information. It is used by authorised Maya Computers staff only and is not a public service.

What we collect

Staff accounts: name, username and an encrypted password. Customers: name and phone number, as entered by staff. Business records: sales, items bought, debts, payments, stock, expenses, and a log of who did what and when.

Why we collect it

To keep accurate shop records, track money owed to the shop, issue receipts, and keep an audit trail. We do not sell or share this information for advertising.

Where it is stored

Records are stored in a cloud database and served by a cloud host. A working copy is also kept on the device a staff member uses, so the system works without internet and syncs later.

Who can see it

Only signed-in staff, limited by role. Cashiers cannot see buying prices, expenses or profit. Only the owner can manage staff accounts and read the activity log.

How long we keep it

Business records are kept for as long as the shop needs them for its accounts and tax obligations. Saved records are locked and cannot be edited or deleted, only voided with a recorded reason.

Your rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, you may ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, and ask questions about how it is used. Because business records are locked for accounting integrity, corrections are made by a recorded void and re-entry rather than by erasing history.

Security

Passwords are stored encrypted, access is controlled by role, connections use HTTPS, and every action is written to a tamper-evident activity log. Staff must keep their passwords private and log out of shared devices.
