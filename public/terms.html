These terms apply to anyone who signs in to Maya Computers Books, the shop's internal record keeping system.

Authorised use only

Access is for staff approved by the shop owner. Each person uses their own account and must not share their password. Accounts can be disabled by the owner at any time.

Accuracy and accountability

Staff must record sales, payments, stock and expenses honestly and accurately. Every action is logged with the user's name and time, and the log cannot be edited.

Locked records

Once saved, records cannot be edited or deleted. A mistaken sale can be voided by a manager or owner with a written reason, then recorded again correctly. Voids are permanent and visible in the log.

Offline use

Entries made without internet are kept on the device and sent to the server when the connection returns. The server may reject an entry if it breaks a rule, for example a payment larger than the balance. Rejected entries are shown on screen and must be checked and re-entered. Staff should not clear browser data or log out while entries are waiting to sync.

Debts

Debts recorded in the system are the shop's record of money owed. The system is a record keeping tool and does not by itself create a legal agreement with a customer.

Availability

We work to keep the system available but do not guarantee uninterrupted service. The shop should keep regular backups.
